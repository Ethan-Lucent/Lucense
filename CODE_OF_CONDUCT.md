
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Lucense is an open-source project built around collaboration, curiosity, and the freedom to create and use software.

We want this project to be a place where people can contribute, learn, disagree, and exchange ideas without unnecessary hostility.

Participation in the Lucense community is open to everyone, regardless of experience level, background, nationality, identity, or technical ability.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Communicate honestly and in good faith.
- Accept reasonable criticism and differing opinions.
- Respect other contributors' time and effort.
- Focus discussions on ideas, implementations, and technical decisions.
- Provide useful feedback without personal attacks.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for newcomers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats.
- Personal attacks, targeted insults, or sustained hostility.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Unwanted sexual attention or harassment.
- Retaliation against anyone reporting misconduct.
- Spam or malicious behavior.

Technical disagreement, criticism, and debate are welcome. Disagreement alone is not misconduct.

## Scope

This code applies to all Lucense project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and code reviews.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where someone is representing the Lucense project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, responses may include:

1. A warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Rejection of contributions.
5. Permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers reserve the right to take appropriate action when necessary.

## Reporting

Report violations directly to the project maintainer:

- GitHub: https://github.com/Ethan-Lucent
- Telegram: https://t.me/Eth4wn

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

## Final Note

Lucense is a software project, not a place for personal conflicts.

Be curious. Be constructive. Respect other people.

Thank you for being part of the community.
